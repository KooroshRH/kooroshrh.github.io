Interests

My recent work is on generative models, mainly diffusion transformers for image generation. I am interested in high-resolution synthesis, efficient inference, and training-free methods that get more out of large pretrained models.

My Ph.D. work with Atena Roshan Fekr and Alex Mihailidis at the University of Toronto uses mmWave radar, RGB-D cameras, and wearable sensors to recognize caregiver activities and reduce infection risk in hospitals.
